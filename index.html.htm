📖 What is Cyber Security Awareness?

Cyber Security Awareness means understanding online threats and learning how to protect our personal information, devices and data while using the internet.


🛡️ Important Cyber Safety Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Never share your OTP or password.
  • Avoid clicking unknown links.
  • Do not download files from untrusted websites.
  • Check website addresses before entering information.
  • Keep your mobile and computer software updated.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Avoid sensitive activities on public Wi-Fi.
  • Report suspicious online activity.

⚠️ Common Cyber Threats

1. Phishing: Fake emails or messages that try to steal personal information.
2. Hacking: Unauthorized access to a computer or account.
3. Malware: Harmful software that can damage devices.
4. Online Scams: Fake offers or messages designed to trick people.
5. Identity Theft: Using another person's personal information without permission.

💡 Remember

Your online safety is in your hands. Be alert, think carefully before clicking, and protect your digital identity.
